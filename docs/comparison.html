Key Findings

1. Frozen encoders with linear probes dramatically outperform training from scratch on high-dimensional environments (Humanoid, Human Motion), achieving copy ratios well below 1.0 where scratch training fails.

2. SIGReg regularization matters. The probe with sr=0.5 consistently matches or approaches the best-tuned regularization strength, suggesting it is a robust default.

3. Low-dimensional environments (PushT, Reacher) are solvable even by small ViT-Tiny models trained from scratch, confirming they serve as useful baselines but do not stress-test the approach.
